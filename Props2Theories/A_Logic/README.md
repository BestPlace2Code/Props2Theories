# 1. Logic

[Natural Deduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_deduction#Gentzen-style_propositional_logic) is one of the most common and convinient underlying systems for formal proof in Math.

In my `TacticNames.lean` file, I defined useful list of tactics
for dealing with Natural Deduction logic.

They reflect the theoretically defined rules in `Natural Deduction`.

You don't need to open and study this file, I will teach you all of them in the 3 chapters of this subject:

- `A_Props` - prove theorems about `Propositional Logic`
- `B_Quants`- prove theorems about `High Order Logic of Predicates with Quantifiers`
- `C_Equality` - prove theorems about `Normal Equality`

To fully understand Logic, the rule foundations of the math, solve all of this chapters, one by one!
